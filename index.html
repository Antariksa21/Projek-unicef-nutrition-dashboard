Clinical Reference Framework

WHO Child Growth Standards & Z-Score Methodology

The World Health Organization (WHO) Child Growth Standards were developed through the Multicentre Growth Reference Study (MGRS), following children raised under optimal socio-economic and nutritional conditions worldwide.

Height-for-Age (HAZ / TB/U)

Measures linear growth failure and cumulative growth deficits over time, indicating chronic malnutrition.

  • < -3 SD Severely Stunted (Sangat Pendek)
  • -3 SD to < -2 SD Stunted (Pendek)
  • -2 SD to +2 SD Normal Linear Growth
  • > +2 SD Tall (Tinggi)

Weight-for-Age (WAZ / BB/U)

Composite index of both acute (wasting) and chronic (stunting) nutritional status. Widely used in community Posyandu growth monitoring.

  • < -3 SD Severely Underweight (Gizi Buruk)
  • -3 SD to < -2 SD Underweight (Gizi Kurang)
  • -2 SD to +2 SD Normal Weight
  • > +2 SD Risk of Overweight

Weight-for-Height (WHZ / BB/TB)

Measures body mass relative to body length. Reflects current nutritional status and acute severe wasting / dehydration.

  • < -3 SD Severely Wasted (Gizi Buruk Akut)
  • -3 SD to < -2 SD Wasted / Thin (Gizi Kurang)
  • -2 SD to +1 SD Normal / Good Nutrition
  • > +2 SD Overweight / Obese
Window of Opportunity

The 1,000 First Days of Life (1000 HPK)

The period from conception (270 days) through a child's second birthday (730 days) is the most critical window to prevent permanent cognitive, metabolic, and physical growth faltering.

Stage 1: Pregnancy (270 Days)

Maternal nutrition, iron-folic acid supplementation (TTD 90+ tablets), and antenatal care visits (ANC 6x).

Stage 2: 0–6 Months (180 Days)

Early Initiation of Breastfeeding (IMD) within 1 hour of birth and Exclusive Breastfeeding without supplementary water or food.

Stage 3: 6–24 Months (550 Days)

Timely, adequate, safe Complementary Feeding (MP-ASI) rich in animal protein (eggs, fish, meat) alongside continued breastfeeding.
